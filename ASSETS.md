# Asset sources

Initial assets retrieved 5 September 2026; wider Nord-Est club directory checked 6 September 2026.

## Club logos and directory

The selector contains the 39 club pages linked by the [FFHG Zone Nord-Est directory](https://nord-est.ffhg.org/annuaire-clubs/). This covers the federation directory, not every independent leisure association, historical club, or age-category squad. Logos identify their respective clubs; no endorsement or ownership of these marks is claimed.

Boulogne ACBB is excluded: its stale individual directory page remains online, but it is absent from the current regional index and its hockey section ceased activity in 2024 ([L’Équipe report](https://www.lequipe.fr/Hockey-sur-glace/Actualites/L-equipe-de-hockey-sur-glace-de-l-acbb-disparait/1526294)).

The Français Volants theme uses navy and white. Navy `#000034` was sampled from the official FFHG-hosted club logo, not from a published brand guideline. The theme uses CSS colors; both views retain club logos and any uploaded background.

## Horn clips

Three CC0 sound files replace the previous crowd recording and synthesized triad. The source pages make the following descriptions; these are alternatives for a hockey scoreboard, not recordings of specific NHL team horns.

| App choice | Source | Author | Source description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deep ship horn (default) | [powerfull horn](https://freesound.org/people/trezz77/sounds/546528/) | trezz77 | Recorded on an icebreaker |
| Industrial air horn | [Industrial Air Horn](https://freesound.org/people/mcpable/sounds/131930/) | mcpable | Air horn on an oil rig in Alberta |
| Short horn with echo | [airhorn-short.wav](https://freesound.org/people/guitarguy1985/sounds/68999/) | guitarguy1985 | Designed short air-horn effect with echo |

Each source page lists [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). High-quality MP3 previews exposed on those pages were downloaded, converted to mono at 44.1 kHz / 160 kbps, trimmed at the start to remove leading quiet, normalized, and limited to 3.2 seconds. The player applies short fades. Junior shifts use the first 0.9 seconds. No crowd track or music is mixed in, and there is no oscillator-generated fallback. A decoding failure displays an audio error.

- `icebreaker.mp3`: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/546/546528_5637404-hq.mp3
- `industrial.mp3`: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/131/131930_1542102-hq.mp3
- `echo.mp3`: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/68/68999_533680-hq.mp3

## Youth categories

U7, U9, U11, U13, U15, U18 and U20 follow the [FFHG tournament list](https://www.hockeyfrance.com/clubs-et-licencies/tournois-et-matchs-amicaux/liste-des-tournois/) as consulted on 5 September 2026. Club/category combinations are user-configurable labels, not verified season registrations. Squad and alliance names remain free text, because participating clubs and categories can change by season.
